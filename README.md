# Small Business AI Ops

Repository for building an AI-assisted service business focused on:
1. small business routine automation
2. document cleanup and repackaging
3. future niche AI assistants

## Initial strategy
Start with automation-first services for small businesses.
After validating recurring workflows, expand into document services.
Then productize repeated patterns into a niche AI assistant.

## Core phases
1. Automation Foundation
2. Workflow Standardization
3. Service Packaging
4. Pilot Delivery
5. Document Expansion
6. Unified AI Operations Layer
7. Assistant Productization

## Repo structure
- `PROJECT_SCOPE.md` — business scope and strategic boundaries
- `TARGET_CLIENTS.md` — target client segments and priorities
- `GEMINI.md` — project operating instructions for Gemini CLI
- `offers/` — packaged offers
- `playbooks/` — standard workflows
- `prompts/` — reusable generation prompts
- `templates/` — reusable deliverable templates
- `ops/` — pricing, QA, delivery, and operating rules

## First milestone
Acquire 3–5 pilot clients in one or two small business segments and deliver:
- lead intake automation
- follow-up automation
- reminders
- daily summaries
- structured response templates

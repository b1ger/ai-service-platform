# Project Scope

## Vision
Build a service-led AI operations business for small businesses that starts with routine automation, expands into document repackaging, and later evolves into niche AI assistants.

## Primary business objective
Help small businesses reduce manual routine work, respond faster to clients, avoid losing leads, and create cleaner internal processes.

## Phase order
1. Automation-first services for small businesses
2. Document cleanup and repackaging as an upsell / expansion
3. Productization into a niche AI assistant based on repeated patterns

## In scope for Phase 1
- lead intake workflows
- inbound message classification
- response draft generation
- reminder workflows
- follow-up workflows
- daily lead summaries
- simple operational documentation for the client
- reusable response templates
- client onboarding questionnaires
- workflow mapping for one business process at a time

## In scope for Phase 2
- commercial proposal drafting
- messy notes to structured documents
- voice transcript to clean summary
- technical text to client-facing document
- reusable document templates
- executive summaries

## In scope for Phase 3
- one narrow assistant for a single repeated use case
- assistant blueprint
- input schema
- output schema
- workflow design
- implementation recommendation

## Out of scope for now
- full custom SaaS platform
- broad CRM replacement
- advanced multi-system integrations
- universal AI assistant for every business
- complex enterprise workflows
- deep legal/compliance automation
- custom mobile apps
- large refactors unrelated to first monetization

## Initial target outcome
Get first paying clients with a simple, understandable offer:
"we help small businesses stop losing leads and reduce routine work."

## Success criteria for initial stage
- 3 to 5 pilot clients
- 2 to 3 repeatable automation packages
- standardized onboarding flow
- standardized delivery artifacts
- reusable prompt and template library
- documented repeated pain points that can inform future productization

## Risks
- trying to serve too many niches too early
- overbuilding before real client feedback
- unclear deliverables
- relying on AI outputs without QA
- promising fully autonomous automation too soon

## Strategic principles
- service first
- narrow scope
- reusable workflows
- manual QA on every delivery
- productize only after repeated real-world usage

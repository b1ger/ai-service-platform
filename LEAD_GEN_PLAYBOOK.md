# Lead Generation Playbook

## Goal
Find small businesses that are likely to benefit from routine automation.

## Good signals
- heavy use of messaging for inquiries
- slow or inconsistent response patterns
- owner-led communication
- appointment-based operations
- repetitive FAQ-style interactions
- visible request for quotes or estimates

## Prospect categories
### Strong fit
- contractors
- custom furniture workshops
- architecture/interior studios
- local clinics
- salons / wellness businesses
- other service businesses with recurring inbound requests

### Medium fit
- small ecommerce brands with manual support
- local agencies with client intake chaos
- professional services with repetitive inquiry flows

## Discovery checklist
- Do they receive inbound requests often?
- Do they reply manually?
- Do they lose context in chats?
- Do they follow up inconsistently?
- Is there one workflow that obviously repeats?

## First qualification outcome
Classify prospects as:
- pilot candidate
- needs nurturing
- weak fit
- not a fit

## Notes rule
Always log one specific observed pain point for every prospect.

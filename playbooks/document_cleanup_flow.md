# Playbook: Document Cleanup Flow

## Goal
Turn raw, messy, or overly technical materials into clean, structured documents.

## Typical sources
- rough notes
- transcripts
- chat threads
- draft proposals
- internal technical descriptions

## Steps
1. gather source material
2. identify document purpose
3. remove noise and duplication
4. preserve core facts
5. organize information into a clear structure
6. rewrite into clean business language
7. create a short summary
8. identify missing facts or assumptions

## Outputs
- structured document
- client-facing version if needed
- short summary
- missing information list

## Edge cases
- contradictory facts
- missing numbers
- unclear commitments
- too much internal jargon

## Acceptance criteria
- no invented facts
- better readability than source
- structure is immediately usable
- summary is short and decision-friendly

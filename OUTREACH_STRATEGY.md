# Outreach Strategy

## Goal
Get the first 3 to 5 pilot clients for routine automation services in one or two small business segments.

## Primary objective
Start conversations with owners or managers who clearly experience repetitive communication chaos and lost leads.

## Recommended initial channels
- personal network
- local business referrals
- Instagram direct messages
- Facebook local business groups
- LinkedIn outreach
- Telegram or local entrepreneur chats

## Best entry positioning
Do not pitch "AI automation" first.

Lead with business value:
- fewer lost leads
- faster replies
- less manual routine
- better follow-up
- more organized client communication

## Offer framing
A strong first line:
"I help small businesses fix one repetitive communication workflow so they stop losing leads and spending time on the same manual replies."

## Initial outreach rule
Keep the first message short.
Focus on one visible pain point.
Ask for a short conversation or offer a simple workflow review.

## Weekly outreach target
- 20 to 30 direct outreach messages
- 5 to 10 follow-ups
- 2 to 3 discovery calls or message-based discoveries
- 1 pilot proposal target

## What to track
- segment
- outreach channel
- response status
- pain point mentioned
- follow-up date
- pilot opportunity level

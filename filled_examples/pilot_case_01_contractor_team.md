# Pilot Case — Contractor Team

## Client overview
- Client name: Serhii
- Business name: RenovPro Team
- Segment: renovation / contractor team
- Team size: 4
- Main contact: owner
- Contact channel: Instagram + phone

## Current-state problem
- Most new inquiries arrive through Instagram DMs.
- The owner replies manually between jobs.
- Important details are often missing from the first message.
- Follow-up on estimate requests happens inconsistently.
- Old inquiries disappear in chat history.

## Workflow selected for pilot
- Workflow name: new inquiry + estimate follow-up
- Why this workflow first: it directly affects incoming work and is highly repetitive
- Expected business value: fewer missed estimate requests and less manual back-and-forth

## Inputs
- Source of inbound requests: Instagram, calls
- Current tools used: Instagram, notes app, phone calls
- Current communication channels: DM, phone
- Existing templates or scripts: none

## Proposed automation logic
1. Turn every inbound message into a structured lead summary.
2. Classify the inquiry as estimate request, general question, weak fit, or urgent request.
3. Send a short first-response template asking for missing details.
4. Trigger a follow-up message if the person stops replying.
5. Include all open inquiries in a daily owner summary.

## Deliverables
- inquiry intake summary template
- first-response template
- estimate follow-up template
- daily inquiry summary template
- workflow notes for owner use

## Risks / constraints
- missing information: job photos and measurements are often absent
- limitations: owner still needs to approve serious quote conversations
- approval dependencies: final estimate remains manual

## Delivery notes
- what was delivered: workflow map, message templates, follow-up logic, daily summary format
- what required manual editing: wording tone adjusted to sound more natural for construction clients
- what should be standardized next: quote-ready intake checklist

## Feedback
- client reaction: liked the clarity and the idea of not forgetting follow-ups
- useful feedback: first message should sound practical, not corporate
- objections: none on the pilot workflow
- follow-up opportunity: proposal prep template and estimate intake form

## Reusable learnings
- repeated pain point: missing job details at first contact
- reusable template candidate: clarification request for dimensions, photos, location, timing
- possible productization signal: intake assistant for renovation estimate requests

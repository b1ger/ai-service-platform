# Acceptance Criteria

## Purpose
Define what “good enough to use” means for the main artifact types.

## Discovery Summary
A discovery summary is acceptable when:
- the main pain point is clear
- the current workflow is understandable
- the repeated tasks are listed
- one strongest pilot candidate is identified
- the next step is obvious
- no business details are invented

## Workflow Map
A workflow map is acceptable when:
- the trigger is clear
- the steps are sequenced logically
- outputs are explicit
- decision points exist where needed
- manual review boundaries are stated
- at least two realistic edge cases are listed

## Proposal Draft
A proposal draft is acceptable when:
- one workflow is clearly identified
- included and excluded scope are explicit
- expected outcome is understandable
- wording is business-readable
- next step is singular and practical
- no vague promises dominate the document

## Delivery Pack
A delivery pack is acceptable when:
- the current issue is summarized clearly
- the workflow is understandable to a non-technical owner
- included templates are named
- usage notes are practical
- exceptions are listed
- next-step recommendation is useful

## Outreach Message
An outreach message is acceptable when:
- it is short
- it reflects one visible pain point
- it offers one clear CTA
- it avoids hype language
- it feels natural enough to send directly

## Reusable Template
A template is acceptable when:
- it can be reused across more than one client
- it does not contain client-specific hidden assumptions
- the structure is clear
- the wording is adaptable

# Productization Checklist

## Demand check
- Has this workflow appeared across multiple client cases?
- Is the pain point strong enough?
- Would a client pay for a narrower, repeatable solution?

## Workflow stability
- Are the steps mostly repeatable?
- Are the required inputs known?
- Are exceptions manageable?

## Output stability
- Is the output structure predictable?
- Is manual editing limited?
- Can QA rules be defined clearly?

## Safety and risk
- Can missing facts be flagged safely?
- Are there claims or commitments that require human review?
- Is the workflow narrow enough to avoid confusion?

## Implementation readiness
- Could this start as an internal tool first?
- Does it need a form, chat flow, or simple app?
- What is the smallest useful version?

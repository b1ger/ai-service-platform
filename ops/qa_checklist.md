# QA Checklist

## Structural QA
- Is the output complete?
- Are all sections present?
- Is the format consistent?

## Business QA
- Is the workflow practical?
- Does the message tone fit a real client conversation?
- Are assumptions clearly labeled?
- Is anything invented?

## Delivery QA
- Can this be sent to the client as-is?
- Is the next step obvious?
- Is the value easy to understand?

# Proposal Pack Checklist

## Proposal pack contents
- short problem summary
- selected workflow
- scope of work
- deliverables
- expected outcome
- timeline or sequence
- next step

## Quality check
- is the workflow specific?
- is the scope narrow enough?
- is the value easy to understand?
- are there any vague promises?
- are manual review needs clear?

## Reuse check
- can this proposal become a template later?
- what parts are niche-specific?
- what parts are reusable across clients?

# Revision Rules

## Goal
Prevent uncontrolled iterations and keep deliverables consistent.

## General rules
- revise based on concrete feedback
- do not rewrite the whole artifact unless necessary
- preserve reusable structure where possible
- record repeated revision patterns in templates or playbooks

## Proposal revisions
Revise for:
- scope clarity
- tone
- inclusion/exclusion boundaries
- next-step clarity

Do not revise into:
- broad undefined transformation
- multi-workflow package unless approved

## Workflow revisions
Revise for:
- missing steps
- better exception handling
- better manual review boundaries
- clearer outputs

## Template revisions
Revise for:
- tone fit
- brevity
- missing fields
- clarity of CTA or next action

## Logging rule
If the same revision comes up more than twice, update the reusable source file.

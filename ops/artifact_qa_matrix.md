# Artifact QA Matrix

## Discovery Summary
### Check
- Is the pain point specific?
- Is fit assessment grounded?
- Is the next step practical?
### Fail examples
- generic pain language
- invented tools or channels
- no pilot candidate

## Workflow Map
### Check
- Is the workflow narrow?
- Are outputs defined?
- Is manual review visible?
### Fail examples
- broad “automation” with no trigger
- no exceptions
- no owner/admin role

## Proposal Draft
### Check
- Is scope narrow enough?
- Are exclusions clear?
- Is the value understandable?
### Fail examples
- everything-included wording
- no exclusions
- hype-driven copy

## Delivery Pack
### Check
- Would a client understand it?
- Are templates and usage notes clear?
- Are human review boundaries included?
### Fail examples
- internal jargon
- no practical next step
- too much technical detail

## Outreach
### Check
- Is it concise?
- Is one pain point visible?
- Is CTA singular?
### Fail examples
- overly long pitch
- several asks in one message
- too much AI language

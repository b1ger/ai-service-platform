# Pricing Notes

## Goal
Keep early pricing simple and tied to one clear workflow.

## Suggested pricing structure
- Mini package: one workflow, simple setup, low-friction pilot price
- Standard package: multiple connected workflows
- Extended package: richer workflow + support + iteration

## Pricing principles
- price by business value, not by number of prompts
- avoid underpricing custom logic
- keep pilot pricing time-bounded
- always define revision limits

## Do not do
- unlimited revisions by default
- vague all-in promises
- fixed price without defined scope

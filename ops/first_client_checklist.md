# First Client Checklist

## Before outreach
- choose one or two target segments
- finalize starter offer
- prepare outreach template
- prepare discovery template
- prepare workflow review template
- prepare pilot proposal structure

## Before discovery
- review the business type
- define likely workflow pain points
- prepare 3 to 5 practical questions
- decide what a small pilot would look like

## Before delivery
- confirm exact workflow scope
- confirm what stays manual
- confirm deliverables
- confirm tone and communication style

## After delivery
- request feedback
- document manual corrections
- update templates
- update playbooks
- record productization hints

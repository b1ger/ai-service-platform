# Document Service Scope

## Purpose
Expand the automation-first service into document cleanup and repackaging once recurring document pain appears in client work.

## Why this is Phase 2
After routine automation is in place, document problems become easier to spot:
- messy proposals
- chaotic notes
- unclear summaries
- technical text that clients cannot easily understand
- repeated document drafting work

## Core service promise
Turn raw, messy, or overly technical materials into clean, structured, client-ready documents.

## In scope
- rough notes to clean document
- transcript to summary
- technical text to client-facing version
- proposal cleanup
- reusable document templates
- executive summary generation

## Typical inputs
- chat threads
- rough notes
- voice transcripts
- old proposals
- email threads
- bullet lists
- technical descriptions

## Typical outputs
- polished document
- client-facing rewrite
- short summary
- missing facts checklist
- reusable template

## Delivery standard
Every document output should be:
- clear
- structured
- fact-preserving
- easy to send
- easy to update later

## Upsell trigger
Offer document service when:
- the client repeatedly sends unstructured notes
- proposals are slow to produce
- staff write inconsistent documents
- owners need summaries instead of raw text

# Discovery Offer

## Purpose
Offer a small paid or low-friction first step before a larger automation engagement.

## Core promise
We review one repetitive communication process and show how it can be simplified.

## Deliverables
- current-state summary
- pain point diagnosis
- workflow map
- recommended automation logic
- next-step recommendation

## Why it works
Some small businesses are not ready to commit to implementation immediately, but they will pay for clarity.

## Best use
- first contact
- uncertain-fit leads
- clients who need to see structure before buying a package

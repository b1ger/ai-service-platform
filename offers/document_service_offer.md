# Document Service Offer

## Working title
Document Cleanup and Repackaging

## Core promise
We turn messy notes, transcripts, and technical text into clear, client-ready documents.

## What we solve
- chaotic raw notes
- badly structured proposals
- technical language that clients do not understand
- missing summaries for managers or owners

## Typical deliverables
- cleaned structured document
- client-facing version
- short executive summary
- reusable template
- recommended follow-up message

## Good upsell path
Offer this service to existing automation clients once recurring document pain becomes visible.

## Typical source materials
- chat threads
- voice transcripts
- bullet notes
- old proposals
- rough technical descriptions

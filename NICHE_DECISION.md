# Niche Decision

## Current decision
Start with small businesses that have frequent inbound requests and repetitive manual communication.

## Initial niche focus
Primary focus:
- renovation / contractor teams
- furniture makers / custom workshops

Secondary candidates:
- architecture / interior studios
- local service businesses with appointments

## Why this starting niche
- high volume of repetitive client questions
- many leads arrive through chat or informal channels
- intake is often chaotic
- follow-up is often inconsistent
- owners are close to operations and feel the pain directly
- document services can later be added naturally

## Decision criteria
The first niche should have:
- clear inbound workflow pain
- clear value from saving time
- simple automation opportunities
- low technical expectations
- repeatable communication patterns

## What success looks like
- 3 pilot cases completed
- repeated workflow patterns identified
- one strong before/after case
- reusable templates built from real work

## Expansion rule
Do not expand niche focus until the first 3–5 cases are delivered and documented.

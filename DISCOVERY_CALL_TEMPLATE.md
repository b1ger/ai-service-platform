# Discovery Call Template

## Goal
Understand one concrete workflow problem and determine whether a pilot is a good fit.

## Opening
- What kind of inquiries do you receive most often?
- How do you usually handle them now?

## Current-state questions
- Where do requests usually come in?
- Who responds?
- What gets repeated most often?
- What gets missed or delayed?
- Do you have any templates or is everything manual?

## Workflow questions
- If we improve just one workflow first, which one matters most?
- What should happen automatically?
- What should stay human-reviewed?

## Outcome questions
- What would make this feel successful for you?
- What would save the most time this month?

## Fit decision
- clear pain?
- repeatable workflow?
- realistic pilot scope?
- willing to test?

## Closing
- summarize the main pain point
- propose one pilot workflow
- explain expected deliverables
- define next step

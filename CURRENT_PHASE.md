# CURRENT_PHASE

## Current phase
Phase 1 — Automation-First Service Delivery

## Phase objective
Acquire and deliver the first 3–5 pilot client cases for routine automation services aimed at small businesses.

## Core business promise
Help small businesses:
- reduce repetitive manual communication
- stop losing leads
- follow up more consistently
- create more structured intake and reminder workflows

## Current scope
In scope right now:
- discovery
- outreach
- workflow mapping
- proposal drafting
- message templates
- follow-up and reminder logic
- daily summary formats
- delivery packs
- pilot documentation

## Explicitly out of scope right now
- large application architecture
- full SaaS product planning
- advanced integrations
- broad document-service expansion
- generic assistant design
- enterprise workflows

## Primary target segments
Current likely best-fit segments:
- renovation / contractor teams
- custom furniture workshops

Secondary possibilities:
- clinics
- other appointment-based local service businesses

## Required outputs in this phase
- outreach messages
- discovery summaries
- workflow maps
- pilot proposals
- delivery documents
- reusable templates
- documented learnings from pilot work

## Success criteria
This phase is successful when:
- 3 to 5 pilot cases are documented
- one starter workflow offer is repeatable
- one or two niches show clear fit
- templates become reusable with limited revision
- repeated pain points are visible enough to guide the next phase

## Gate to next phase
Move to broader document-service work only when:
- pilot delivery is stable
- repeated document pain appears in real client cases
- templates and workflow outputs are already reliable

## Current execution rule
Bias toward shipping client-facing artifacts and learning from real workflow cases.
Do not bias toward building software prematurely.

# Target Clients

## Primary target for Phase 1
Small businesses with:
- frequent inbound inquiries
- repetitive client communication
- disorganized lead handling
- no structured intake process
- no standard follow-up process
- low operational maturity but clear business value from saving time

## Priority segments
1. renovation / contractor teams
2. furniture makers / custom furniture workshops
3. architecture / interior design studios
4. local beauty or wellness businesses
5. dental or medical private practices
6. local service businesses with appointment requests

## Why these segments
- they often rely on messaging and informal communication
- they frequently lose information in chats
- they need faster client response
- they often create repetitive offers or explanations
- they usually do not have polished internal operations

## Buyer personas
### Owner-operator
- overwhelmed by daily communication
- answers clients personally
- loses leads in chats
- wants simple systems, not technical complexity

### Small team manager
- needs intake clarity
- wants staff to reply consistently
- needs reminders and summaries
- wants less chaos and fewer missed messages

## Core pains
- slow first response
- repeated manual typing
- forgotten follow-ups
- scattered client details
- unclear prioritization of inquiries
- weak handoff between team members

## Entry positioning
Do not sell "AI".
Sell:
- fewer lost leads
- faster replies
- more organized inbound requests
- less manual routine
- better daily visibility

## Segment selection rule
Start with one or two segments only.
Do not expand until at least 3 real pilot cases are delivered and documented.

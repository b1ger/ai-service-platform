# Discovery Summary Prompt

## Goal
Convert raw prospect notes into a structured discovery summary for service qualification.

## Input
- raw notes or transcript excerpt
- prospect segment
- communication channel
- observed pain points
- likely workflow candidate

## Required output
- prospect section
- observed pain point
- current workflow summary
- repeated tasks
- strongest pilot candidate
- fit assessment
- next step

## Constraints
- do not invent company details
- do not overstate fit
- keep the workflow candidate narrow
- highlight missing inputs if the notes are weak

## Acceptance criteria
- another operator can read the summary and understand what to propose next
